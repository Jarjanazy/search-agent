# Syrian Olive Oil — Weekly Business Intelligence Report

## Executive Summary

Syrian olive oil production faced significant challenges in 2025, with output estimates ranging from 412,000 to 600,000 tons of olives yielding approximately 66,000 tons of oil, down from historical averages due to climate change, fires, and weak service infrastructure. The 2026 season shows promising recovery signs with olive trees entering bloom phase. Domestic prices range from 65–75,000 Syrian pounds per kilogram, while export-grade oil trades at $85–100 USD per 16-kilogram canister. Export activity is expanding, with packaged olive oil exports rising 69% through March 2026, though Syria relies on neighboring countries like Turkey for transit logistics.
