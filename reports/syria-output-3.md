# Syrian Olive Oil — Weekly Business Intelligence Report

## Executive Summary

Syria's 2026 olive season represents an average-to-weak production outlook, with [the Ministry of Agriculture expecting 412,000 tons of olives to yield approximately 65,000 tons of oil](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). Export pricing has increased significantly, with [olive oil prices expected to exceed $8 per liter in 2026, up from under $6 per liter in the previous year](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). Regional production spans Aleppo, the coastal region, and Homs. [One major exporter, Al Reef Company, shipped 500 tons primarily to Gulf countries but cannot predict 2026 export quantities](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). Syria's post-sanctions environment has created some openness to export discussions, though official government export policies remain unclear.
