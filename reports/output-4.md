# Syrian Olive Oil Weekly Business Intelligence Report

## Executive Summary

Syria's 2026 olive season faces significant headwinds, with the Ministry of Agriculture forecasting an average-to-weak harvest of [approximately 412,000 tons of olives yielding an estimated 65,000 tons of oil](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/), down from higher expectations. The sector is under acute environmental stress due to [record drought—the worst in 60 years](https://syriadirect.org/devastated-by-drought-daraas-olive-harvest-hits-a-new-low/)—while water scarcity and climate shocks threaten long-term viability. Prices have risen sharply to [over $8 per liter](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/) domestically, and export volumes remain limited due to ongoing market constraints.

## Key Findings

**PRODUCTION**

- [2026 forecast: 65,000 tons of olive oil from 412,000 tons of olives—classified as an average-to-weak season by the Ministry of Agriculture](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/)
- [Regional breakdown for previous season: Homs produced 47,556 tons of olives; Damascus countryside 40,277 tons; Idlib 35,534 tons; Aleppo 33,794 tons; Tartous 32,034 tons; Daraa 28,673 tons](https://sana.sy/en/?p=335186)
- [Daraa province experienced a 68% harvest decline compared to 2024 and 86% decline compared to 2011, driven by severe drought](https://syriadirect.org/devastated-by-drought-daraas-olive-harvest-hits-a-new-low/)
- [Coastal olive production has fallen by nearly 50% over the past two decades due to recurring droughts and reduced rainfall](https://carnegieendowment.org/sada/2026/04/syria-on-the-brink-of-water-scarcity-climate-change-drought-and-threats-to-food-security)

**PRICES & TRADE**

- [Domestic wholesale olive oil prices have risen from a maximum of $6 per liter last year to an expected $8+ per liter in 2026](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/)
- [Al Reef Company, a major Syrian olive oil exporter, exported 500 tons in the prior year, primarily to Gulf countries, but cannot predict export volume for 2026](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/)
- [Emerging export interest in Eastern Europe, particularly Russia, Ukraine, and Poland](https://alameedoils.com/en/syrian-olive-oil-guide/)
- [Syria ranks as the third-largest Middle East/Mediterranean supplier to Saudi Arabia after Tunisia and Spain](https://indexbox.io/store/saudi-arabia-virgin-olive-oil-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/)

**MARKET OPPORTUNITIES**

- [The FAO has completed a comprehensive value chain study addressing production, quality control mechanisms, and international export positioning](https://www.fao.org/syria/news/details/fao--ministry-of-agriculture--and-italy-organize-roundtable-on-the-future-of-syria-s-olive-sector/en)
