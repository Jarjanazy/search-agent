# Syrian Olive Oil: Weekly Business Intelligence Report

## Executive Summary

[Syria's 2025 olive harvest declined sharply to an estimated 412,000–600,000 tons of olives due to climate change, fires, and weak services](<https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/>), a significant contraction from 2023 production of approximately [125,000 tons of oil](<https://alpheratzmag.com/reports/20250103003/>). However, [olive trees in Syria began flowering in early 2026, signaling an anticipated abundant season](<https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/>), with government officials forecasting a [6% production increase for the upcoming season](<https://www.syria.tv/tag/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B8%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%B1%D9%89>). Domestic pricing reflects scarcity, with [retail oil prices ranging between 65,000–75,000 Syrian pounds per kilogram](<https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/>).
