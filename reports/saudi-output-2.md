# Saudi Arabia Oil Production — Weekly Business Intelligence Report

Saudi Arabia's oil sector remains in crisis amid an unprecedented 69-day Strait of Hormuz closure following Iran-US-Israel escalation. [Crude production plummeted from 10.1 million barrels per day (bpd) to 6.8 mbpd in April 2026](https://easternherald.com/2026/05/13/saudi-arabia-oil-output-opec-middle-east-crisis-uae-exit/), representing a 23% decline, forcing heavy reliance on alternate export routes via pipeline to the Red Sea. [Saudi Aramco posted Q1 2026 adjusted net income of $33.6 billion, up 26% year-on-year](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-saudi-aramco-q1-2026-earnings-beat-forecasts-amid-disruptions-93CH-4676866), buoyed by record pricing despite output disruptions. Export flows to China are collapsing, projected to reach just 13–14 million barrels in June, while OPEC+ announced token quota increases even as actual production remains far below allocated ceilings.

## PRODUCTION

**Crude Output & OPEC+ Compliance**

- [Saudi Arabia's crude oil production stood at approximately 10.1 million barrels per day as of early 2026](https://houseofsaud.com/business/oil-industry/), but this figure deteriorated sharply following Strait of Hormuz closure.
- [Production dropped 23% from 10.1 million bpd to 7.8 million bpd in April 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/13/iran-war-oil-opec-production-decline.html).
- [By early May, output had further declined to 6.768 million barrels per day according to OPEC's May 2026 monthly report](https://easternherald.com/2026/05/13/saudi-arabia-oil-output-opec-middle-east-crisis-uae-exit/).
- [Production decreased to 6,879 barrels per day per thousand in April 2026 from 7,763 in March](https://tradingeconomics.com/saudi-arabia/crude-oil-production).
- [In February 2026, total production reached 10.88 million barrels per day, the highest level recorded, representing a 7.7% increase from January 2026](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1898395).

**OPEC+ Quota & Saudi Position**

- [Saudi Arabia's OPEC+ production quota will rise to 10.291 million bpd in June 2026 under OPEC+ agreement, far above actual production](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/3/opec-announces-symbolic-oil-output-rise-during-strait-of-hormuz-closure).
- [Seven major producers, including Saudi Arabia and Russia, will add 188,000 barrels per day to their total production quota for June 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/3/opec-announces-symbolic-oil-output-rise-during-strait-of-hormuz-closure).
- [OPEC+ implemented production adjustments of 206 thousand barrels per day from the 1.65 million barrels per day in additional voluntary adjustments](https://www.opec.org/pr-detail/1756597-5-april-2026.html).
- [The UAE announced its withdrawal from OPEC and OPEC+, dealing a heavy blow to the cartel and its de facto leader, Saudi Arabia](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-cuts-arab-light-june-osp-asia-2026-05-05/).

**Export Pipeline & Alternate Routes**

- [Saudi Aramco requested buyers submit nominations for June-loading crude, including planned lifting volumes from both Ras Tanura export terminal inside the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea port of Yanbu, in case the Strait remains closed](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-cuts-arab-light-june-osp-asia-2026-05-05/).
- [The Saudis are relying on a crucial East-West pipeline to reroute barrels from the Persian Gulf to the Red Sea for export](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/13/iran-war-oil-opec-production-decline.html).

## RISKS & DISRUPTIONS

**Strait of Hormuz Crisis**

- [Tensions between Iran, the United States, and Israel escalated in the lead-up to 2026, stemming from failed nuclear negotiations and prior air conflict, with Iran signaling potential disruptions to the Strait of Hormuz in response to threats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis).
- [On February 28, 2026, US and Israeli forces struck Iran. Within 48 hours, the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil and trade route, was effectively closed](https://carraglobe.com/strait-of-hormuz-closure-2026/).
- [As of early May 2026, the Strait has been effectively closed to commercial shipping for 69 or more days, following an initial closure in March 2026 and a failed brief reopening on April 21–22, 2026 that collapsed within 24 hours](https://discoveryalert.com.au/strait-hormuz-shipping-disruptions-oil-lng-closure-2026/).
- [Hundreds of vessels accumulated in holding patterns across the Gulf of Oman. Approximately 500,000 containers were stranded, with roughly 20,000 seafarers unable to complete their voyages](https://discoveryalert.com.au/strait-hormuz-shipping-disruption-oil-prices-trade-routes-2026/).
- [Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz has ground to a near halt after vessels in the area were hit as Iran retaliated against U.S. and Israeli actions](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/iran-conflict-disrupts-global-shipping-tankers-are-stranded-damaged-2026-03-02/).

**Market Impact**

- [OPEC lowered its demand growth forecast for 2026 to around 1.2 million barrels per day, down from about 1.4 million bpd previously](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/opec-oil-strait-hormuz-iran-war-saudi-arabia-uae.html).
