# Syrian Olive Oil—Weekly Business Intelligence Report

Syria's 2026 olive season shows mixed signals with production expectations significantly lower than prior year, while export momentum faces policy uncertainty and pricing pressures amid rising operational costs.

## PRODUCTION

**Volume Estimates**

[The Ministry of Agriculture anticipates an average to low olive season in 2026: at most 412,000 tons of olives yielding an estimated 65,000 tons of oil](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). This represents a substantial decline from 2025 estimates, which ranged between [412,000 and 600,000 tons of olives](https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/). [The 2025 season witnessed a sharp decline in production due to climate variations, fires, and weak services](https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/), contrasting with early 2026 expectations. As of late March 2026, [olive trees in Syria have begun flowering, signaling a potential abundant harvest compared to previous years](https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/).

**Regional Breakdown**

[The primary oil-pressing companies operate in multiple regions including Aleppo, the Syrian coast, and Homs](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). [Idlib and Hama are noted as traditional olive-producing regions](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). [A harvest event was documented in the Homs countryside in April 2026, after nearly 14 years of civil war disruption](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/29/g-s1-118663/syria-olive-harvest-homs).

## PRICES & TRADE

**Pricing**

[Al Reef Company, a major Syrian olive oil exporter, reported that pricing last year did not exceed six dollars per liter, while this year is expected to reach more than eight dollars per liter](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). [Transaction prices sampled in February 2026 recorded 5.27 USD per kilogram](https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/olive-oil/SY#price). [Locally, prices for olive oil currently range between 65–75,000 Syrian pounds per kilogram, while table olive prices range between 30–45,000 pounds](https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/). [A 16-kilogram container (tank) of olive oil averages between 85–100 USD](https://alameedoils.com/%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%B1-%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7/).

**Export Destinations & Volumes**

[Al Reef Company exported 500 tons primarily to Gulf countries last year](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). [Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Lebanon, India, and the United Arab Emirates are currently the largest destinations for Syrian exports](https://syriacapitalpartners.com/blog/what-syria-emerging-export-markets-outside-middle-east/). [The company said it cannot predict the quantity of exports in 2026](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/).

**Export Policy Status**

[Syria's Ministry of Agriculture did not respond regarding the government's export strategies and plans for 2026](https://syriadirect.org/how-olive-oil-exports-exacerbate-syrias-water-crisis/). [As of March 2026, there are questions about the possibility of allowing olive oil exports to manage farmer surpluses, particularly given rising local prices](https://b2b-sy.com/news/1033576588/).
