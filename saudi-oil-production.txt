Saudi Arabia oil production — weekly business intelligence report. Cover all of the following with current data:

PRODUCTION
- Latest crude oil output (million barrels per day)
- OPEC+ quota compliance and Saudi actual vs. target production
- Year-on-year and month-on-month production changes
- Aramco field-level updates (Ghawar, Khurais, Shaybah, offshore)
- Upstream capacity utilization and spare capacity estimates

PRICES & TRADE
- Saudi Official Selling Price (OSP) for Arab Light, Medium, Heavy to Asia, Europe, US
- Brent and WTI benchmark correlation
- Active export volumes by destination (Asia, Europe, US, India, China)
- Tanker loadings and shipping route updates

OPEC+ & POLICY
- Latest OPEC+ meeting outcomes and Saudi position
- Voluntary cut announcements or rollbacks
- Saudi Energy Ministry statements on production targets
- US-Saudi diplomatic developments affecting energy policy

MARKET & REVENUE
- Saudi Aramco quarterly production and revenue figures
- Vision 2030 energy diversification updates
- Refinery expansion or downstream investment news
- Natural gas production and domestic energy mix shifts

RISKS & DISRUPTIONS
- Geopolitical events in the Gulf affecting shipping lanes (Strait of Hormuz)
- Infrastructure attacks or security incidents
- Demand-side shocks from major importers (China slowdown, recession risk)
- Climate or seasonal factors affecting operations
